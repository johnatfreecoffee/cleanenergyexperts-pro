Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you use our eligibility form, we collect:

  • Name (first and last)

  • Home address
  • Approximate monthly electric bill

We may also collect standard analytics data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Determine your eligibility for Edison's VPP battery program
  • Contact you by phone or text about your eligibility status

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Our energy installation partners (only if you qualify and consent to proceed)
  • Service providers who help us operate our website
  • As required by law

4. Text Messages

By submitting your phone number, you consent to receive text messages from Clean Energy Experts regarding your eligibility and the battery program. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of sales (we don't sell your data). To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@cleanenergyexperts.pro.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@cleanenergyexperts.pro.
