Common Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. Edison's VPP (Virtual Power Plant) program covers the full cost of battery installation for qualifying homes. There are no hidden fees or payments.

What is the VPP program?

Edison's Virtual Power Plant program installs batteries in homes across Southern California. During peak demand, Edison can draw small amounts of stored energy to stabilize the grid — in exchange, you get a free battery and bill credits.

Do I need solar panels?

Not necessarily. Some homes qualify for batteries with or without solar. We'll check your specific situation during the eligibility review.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homeowners across all of Southern California Edison's service territory — that's over 50,000 square miles covering parts of 15 counties. This includes the Inland Empire (Riverside, San Bernardino, Menifee, Temecula, Hemet), Orange County (Anaheim, Irvine, Huntington Beach), parts of Los Angeles County, Ventura County, the High Desert, Coachella Valley, Central Coast, and more. If Edison is your utility, we can help.

How long does installation take?

Most installations are completed in one day. From qualification to install is typically 2-4 weeks depending on permitting in your area.

Who are Clean Energy Experts?

We're a team of local energy consultants specializing in helping Southern California homeowners access clean energy programs. We guide you through the entire process — from eligibility to installation — at no cost to you.

Why are Edison rates going up?

Edison has raised residential rates over 40% in the past five years. Infrastructure upgrades, wildfire liability, and grid maintenance costs are all passed to customers. Home batteries help you reduce your dependence on the grid and protect against future rate increases.
