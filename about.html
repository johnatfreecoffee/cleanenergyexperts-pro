About Clean Energy Experts

We help Edison customers across Southern California take control of their energy — with no cost and no hassle.

Real People. Real Solutions.

We're a team of energy consultants who specialize in one thing: helping Southern California homeowners access the programs that save them the most money.

Edison rates have gone up over 40% in the last five years, and most homeowners have no idea what they're actually paying per kilowatt-hour. We change that.

We guide homeowners through the entire process — from understanding their bill to qualifying for battery programs to installation day. No pressure, no gimmicks. Just honest energy advice from people who live in your community.

What We Do

We make clean energy simple for Southern California homeowners.

Energy Analysis

Upload your power bill and we'll show you your real rate, where your money is going, and what you can do about it. Free, instant, no sign-up required.

Battery Programs

We connect qualifying homeowners with Edison's VPP battery program — a free home battery that provides backup power and lowers your bill.

Solar Optimization

Already have solar? We'll analyze your setup and show you if a battery can capture the energy you're currently sending back to Edison for free.
